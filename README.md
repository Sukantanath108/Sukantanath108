## 👋 About Me

I am a final-year **Mechanical Engineering undergraduate at Chittagong University of Engineering & Technology (CUET)**, interested in the intersection of **Mechanical Engineering, Computer Vision, Data-Driven Engineering, and Intelligent Manufacturing**.

My current interests focus on applying computational methods to physical engineering problems, particularly:

- Industrial Computer Vision & Machine Vision
- Smart Manufacturing & Quality Inspection
- Data-Driven Mechanical Engineering
- Simulation-Assisted Engineering
- Predictive Maintenance
- Digital Twins

I have explored machine learning, deep learning, computer vision, numerical modelling, and software development through academic research, engineering projects, competitions, and personal projects.

### 🔬 Current Work

- **Undergraduate Thesis:** Developing a quality-weighted data synthesis and simulation-guided framework for waste-cooking-oil biodiesel blend screening using **Diesel-RK**
- Building practical experience in **Python, OpenCV, PyTorch, and machine learning**
- Exploring **computer vision applications in manufacturing and mechanical systems**
- Contributing to **CUET Mongolchari – Mars Rover Team**

### 📚 Research

**AJCAI 2026 — Accepted Paper**  
Research in AI and computational modelling.

**Undergraduate Thesis**  
*A Quality-Weighted Framework for Data Synthesis and Simulation-Guided Blend Screening of Waste Cooking Oil Biodiesel Blends*
